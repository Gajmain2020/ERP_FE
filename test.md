- **SplashScreen** (SplashScreen.kt)

  This is the first screen which will be displayed when the app is loading while opening the app.

- **Home** (Home.java)

  The Home Screen offers quick access to useful tools like Age Calculator, Age Difference, Leap Year Checker, and Working Days Between Dates. You can also Add Family & Friends, check Celebrity Birthdays, view your Horoscope, and find Fitness Centers Near You. All features are organized for a smooth and interactive user experience.

- **Age Calculator** (AgeCalc.java)

  The Age Calculator helps you find your exact age by entering your date of birth and today’s date. It shows your age in years, months, and days along with your upcoming birthday. You’ll also see how many days are left until your next birthday.
